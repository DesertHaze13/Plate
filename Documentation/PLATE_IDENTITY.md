# Plate / original identity — build 15

Plate is a daily food record. Its visual language comes from a printed food journal, a nutrition label and the geometry of a plate.

## Visual system

- Cobalt ink is the signature. Warm paper replaces ambient pastel backgrounds. Vermilion and ochre distinguish supporting information; the hero uses lime, white and peach for readable macro slices on cobalt.
- Oversized condensed typography gives the Today masthead a distinct voice: “YOUR DAY, ON A PLATE.” Smaller monospaced indices and thin rules structure the information.
- Solid paper panels with crisp borders replace translucent floating cards. Decorative stacked tiles and ambient gradient circles are removed.
- The plate signature appears in capture, the background’s quiet concentric rules, the selectable macro ring and a new layered P. app icon.

## Layout and interaction

The date selector sits below the masthead, followed by a full-width intake panel. iPhone prioritizes the food journal before target details. iPad and Mac use a broad journal with a narrower target column. Secondary commentary lives under “Read the day.”

The existing interactive pie, macro buttons, food/category detail sheet, historical day selection, Indonesian warnings and cloud recognition remain functional. Native system tabs retain real Liquid Glass behavior. Card surfaces and branding use Plate’s own language.

Press/hover feedback, spring selection, numeric transitions and subtle plate-ring breathing remain. Decorative motion respects Reduce Motion. Light/dark accent colors preserve legibility; the hero remains deep cobalt in both appearances.

This release changes presentation, not the data schema, nutrient arithmetic, account identity or cloud rules. Existing app data and sign-in are retained.
